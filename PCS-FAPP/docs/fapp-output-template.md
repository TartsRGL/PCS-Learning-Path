# FAPP Proposal Output Template

Every proposal generated by FAPP is structured according to the following standard template to ensure maximum readability, clear value framing, and risk mitigation.

---

## Proposal Layout & Sections

### 1. Project Summary
* **Purpose:** A concise executive overview of the project.
* **Content:** 2-3 sentences explaining what is being built, for whom, and why it is important.

### 2. Problem Diagnosis
* **Purpose:** Demonstrates a deep understanding of the client's current pain points.
* **Content:** Identifies bottlenecks, manual processes, or technical limitations currently faced by the client.

### 3. Target State
* **Purpose:** Paints a picture of success post-implementation.
* **Content:** Focuses on the desired end-user experience and operational state.

### 4. Business Value
* **Purpose:** Frames the financial or operational return on investment (ROI).
* **Content:** Quantified impact where possible (e.g., hours saved per week, percentage reduction in errors, potential revenue growth).

### 5. Recommended Scope (MVP)
* **Purpose:** Establishes the exact boundary of what must be built first.
* **Content:** Bulleted list of must-have features required to launch the product.

### 6. What is Outside MVP (Out of Scope)
* **Purpose:** Explicitly prevents scope creep before the project starts.
* **Content:** Bulleted list of nice-to-have features or future integrations that are excluded from this phase.

### 7. Risks and Open Questions
* **Purpose:** Shows technical professionalism and highlights external dependencies.
* **Content:** List of technical uncertainties (e.g., API stability, data cleanliness, third-party approvals) and outstanding design decisions.

### 8. Suggested Next Steps
* **Purpose:** Provides a clear path forward to start the engagement.
* **Content:** Actionable milestones (e.g., kickoff call, technical access, environment setup).

### 9. Pricing Packages
* **Purpose:** Gives the client choice, moving the conversation away from "take it or leave it" pricing.
* **Content:** Grid of three pricing tiers (Essential, Professional, Scale) with distinct scope and pricing points.
