# FAPP Pricing Packages Structure

FAPP uses a three-tier pricing model to frame value and provide options to the end client. This approach shifts the conversation from hourly billing to value-based deliverables.

---

## The Three Packages

### **1. Essential (Tier 1)**
* **Objective:** Solve the core problem with the absolute minimum complexity.
* **Target Audience:** Startups or clients testing a new hypothesis with limited budget.
* **Scope Included:**
  * Core functional MVP flow only.
  * Basic, unpolished UI (standard UI frameworks or minimal custom styles).
  * No complex database migrations or third-party integrations (unless critical).
* **Delivery Time:** Fast (typically 2-4 weeks).
* **Pricing Focus:** Low, entry-level fixed cost.

### **2. Professional (Tier 2) - *Recommended***
* **Objective:** The optimal balance of features, scalability, polish, and UX.
* **Target Audience:** Established businesses looking to deploy a production-grade solution.
* **Scope Included:**
  * All Essential features.
  * Polish and micro-animations (high-quality UI/UX).
  * Standard integrations (e.g., Stripe, analytics, CRM).
  * Comprehensive testing and error handling.
  * 30 days of post-launch maintenance.
* **Delivery Time:** Moderate (typically 4-8 weeks).
* **Pricing Focus:** Medium, value-optimized price reflecting professional service.

### **3. Scale (Tier 3)**
* **Objective:** Provide a high-performance, future-proof solution with maximum support.
* **Target Audience:** Enterprise clients or high-growth projects requiring custom architectures.
* **Scope Included:**
  * All Professional features.
  * High-performance tuning and advanced security layers.
  * Custom database architectures and legacy system integrations.
  * Custom analytics dashboards.
  * 90 days of dedicated post-launch support and optimization SLA.
* **Delivery Time:** Extended (typically 8+ weeks).
* **Pricing Focus:** High, premium value-based pricing.
